# M0 gate: checks by hand

About 20 minutes. Open each link, compare with the official source, and tick the box. If anything is wrong, note the check number and what you saw.

**Reader:** https://kurianjose7586.github.io/git-blame-the-constitution/ (each link below opens one view on one date)

**Official sources to compare against**

- Constitution: [Legislative Department PDF, as on 10 Sep 2026](https://www.legislative.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/95ef471035ee0b5e4e5b1cac3d3584c1.pdf) (downloads; the Preamble is on PDF page 32, Article 15 on pages 37–39, with footnotes at the bottom of each page)
- RTI Act today: [DoPT consolidated text, as on 18 Nov 2025](https://rti.dopt.gov.in/Writereaddata/RTI%20Act,%202005%20(Amended)-English%20Version.PDF)
- RTI Act as enacted: [2005 Gazette copy](https://rti.dopt.gov.in/Writereaddata/RTI-Act.pdf)

## The Constitution

- [ ] 1. [Preamble on 2 Jan 1977](https://kurianjose7586.github.io/git-blame-the-constitution/#/constitution/preamble@1977-01-02) says "SOVEREIGN DEMOCRATIC REPUBLIC" and "unity of the Nation".
- [ ] 2. [Preamble on 3 Jan 1977](https://kurianjose7586.github.io/git-blame-the-constitution/#/constitution/preamble@1977-01-03) says "SOVEREIGN SOCIALIST SECULAR DEMOCRATIC REPUBLIC" and "unity and integrity of the Nation", word for word as in the official PDF.
- [ ] 3. [Preamble, 1950 to today](https://kurianjose7586.github.io/git-blame-the-constitution/#/constitution/preamble@1950-01-26..2026-11-21) shows exactly two changes, both in green.
- [ ] 4. [Article 15 on 17 Jun 1951](https://kurianjose7586.github.io/git-blame-the-constitution/#/constitution/part-iii/article-015@1951-06-17) has clauses (1) to (3) only.
- [ ] 5. [Article 15 on 18 Jun 1951](https://kurianjose7586.github.io/git-blame-the-constitution/#/constitution/part-iii/article-015@1951-06-18) adds clause (4).
- [ ] 6. [On 19 Jan 2006](https://kurianjose7586.github.io/git-blame-the-constitution/#/constitution/part-iii/article-015@2006-01-19) there's no clause (5); [on 20 Jan 2006](https://kurianjose7586.github.io/git-blame-the-constitution/#/constitution/part-iii/article-015@2006-01-20) there is.
- [ ] 7. [On 13 Jan 2019, as in force](https://kurianjose7586.github.io/git-blame-the-constitution/#/constitution/part-iii/article-015@2019-01-13) there's no clause (6); [as published on the same day](https://kurianjose7586.github.io/git-blame-the-constitution/#/constitution/part-iii/article-015@2019-01-13?published) there is (enacted 12 Jan, in force 14 Jan).
- [ ] 8. [Article 15 today](https://kurianjose7586.github.io/git-blame-the-constitution/#/constitution/part-iii/article-015) matches the official PDF word for word, including (6)(a), (6)(b) and the Explanation.
- [ ] 9. [Article 15 blame](https://kurianjose7586.github.io/git-blame-the-constitution/#/constitution/part-iii/article-015?view=blame) has four blocks that match the PDF's footnotes: (4) First Amendment, (5) Ninety-third, (6) onwards One Hundred and Third.
- [ ] 10. [GitHub's own blame](https://github.com/india-law/constitution/blame/main/part-iii/article-015.md) shows the same four acts beside the lines.

## The RTI Act

- [ ] 11. [s. 12 on 1 Jul 2005](https://kurianjose7586.github.io/git-blame-the-constitution/#/rti-act/chapter-iii/section-012@2005-07-01) is in force; [s. 6 on 11 Oct 2005](https://kurianjose7586.github.io/git-blame-the-constitution/#/rti-act/chapter-ii/section-006@2005-10-11) says "Enacted but not in force"; [s. 6 on 12 Oct 2005](https://kurianjose7586.github.io/git-blame-the-constitution/#/rti-act/chapter-ii/section-006@2005-10-12) is in force.
- [ ] 12. [s. 1, 30 to 31 Oct 2019](https://kurianjose7586.github.io/git-blame-the-constitution/#/rti-act/chapter-i/section-001@2019-10-30..2019-10-31) removes "except the State of Jammu and Kashmir".
- [ ] 13. [s. 13, 23 to 24 Oct 2019](https://kurianjose7586.github.io/git-blame-the-constitution/#/rti-act/chapter-iii/section-013@2019-10-23..2019-10-24) changes the term in (1) and (2) and replaces (5). The new text matches s. 2 of the 2019 Act (DoPT's copy adds "(24 of 2019)" twice; the Act doesn't).
- [ ] 14. [s. 16, 23 to 24 Oct 2019](https://kurianjose7586.github.io/git-blame-the-constitution/#/rti-act/chapter-iv/section-016@2019-10-23..2019-10-24): the same for the State Commissions, matching s. 3 of the 2019 Act ("thereunder", where DoPT has "there under").
- [ ] 15. [s. 27 on 24 Oct 2019](https://kurianjose7586.github.io/git-blame-the-constitution/#/rti-act/chapter-vi/section-027@2019-10-24) has the new clauses (ca) and (cb).
- [ ] 16. [s. 8, 12 to 13 Nov 2025](https://kurianjose7586.github.io/git-blame-the-constitution/#/rti-act/chapter-ii/section-008@2025-11-12..2025-11-13) removes the old clause (j) and its proviso and adds the new one-line clause (j). The old text matches the 2005 Gazette and the new matches DoPT's s. 8.
- [ ] 17. [Second Schedule on 1 Oct 2005, as in force](https://kurianjose7586.github.io/git-blame-the-constitution/#/rti-act/schedule-2@2005-10-01) has the original 18 entries ("15. Special Service Bureau", "8. Special Prontier Force"). [As published that day](https://kurianjose7586.github.io/git-blame-the-constitution/#/rti-act/schedule-2@2005-10-01?published) it has "15. Sashtra Seema Bal." and entries 19 to 22 (notification dated 28 Sep, published 8 Oct).
- [ ] 18. [Second Schedule today](https://kurianjose7586.github.io/git-blame-the-constitution/#/rti-act/schedule-2) matches DoPT's: 25 entries, no entry 7. (Entry 27, CERT-In, added by G.S.R. 864(E) in 2023, is missing from DoPT's copy and from ours until we have the notification.)
- [ ] 19. [Second Schedule blame](https://kurianjose7586.github.io/git-blame-the-constitution/#/rti-act/schedule-2?view=blame) gives each entry the notification DoPT's footnotes name (footnotes 1 to 10).

## The reader itself

- [ ] 20. On any Read view, click "Copy a link to this version", paste the link into a new tab: it opens the same provision on the same date.
- [ ] 21. [The RTI Act's contents](https://kurianjose7586.github.io/git-blame-the-constitution/#/rti-act) shows each chapter's title as its heading ("CHAPTER III — THE CENTRAL INFORMATION COMMISSION").
- [ ] 22. Open the reader on your phone: blame and diff are readable without sideways scrolling.
