## 1. Legislative Department (India Code and legislative.gov.in)

**To:** support@indiacode.gov.in (the address India Code lists under Contact Us)
**Subject:** Permission to reproduce Acts from India Code and legislative.gov.in in a non-commercial research project

> Dear Sir/Madam,
>
> I am [your name], a [degree/year] student at [institution], working under [mentor's name, designation] on a non-commercial research project called "git blame the Constitution". It publishes the text of central Acts with their full amendment history, one change per entry, so that anyone can see how each provision read on any date and which amendment changed it. Every page states that the text is unofficial, is not legal advice, and links to the official text.
>
> So far it covers the Preamble and Article 15 of the Constitution and the Right to Information Act, 2005:
>
> - Reader: https://kurianjose7586.github.io/git-blame-the-constitution/
> - Texts: https://github.com/india-law
>
> The texts are reproduced verbatim and attributed. The Constitution text is from "The Constitution of India (as on 10 September 2026)" on legislative.gov.in, and the amending Acts were checked against their copies on India Code and the e-Gazette. The project is a research prototype; it is not sold, and carries no advertising.
>
> Your Copyright Policy asks for permission before material is reproduced, so I would be grateful for:
>
> 1. Permission to reproduce the text of central Acts and of the Constitution, as published on legislative.gov.in and India Code, in this form, with attribution and a link to the official text on every page.
> 2. Confirmation that the IndiaCode Legislative APIs on API Setu may be used for this project, at a modest rate (one request every few seconds), to keep the amendment histories up to date.
>
> While building it, we found a few places where the consolidated texts appear to differ from the Gazette. They are listed below, in case they are useful:
>
> - **RTI Act, s. 8(1)(j):** India Code's text still has the clause as enacted in 2005. Section 44(3) of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 substituted it with effect from 13 November 2025.
> - **RTI Act, Second Schedule:** entry 27, "Indian Computer Emergency Response Team.", inserted by G.S.R. 864(E) of 23 November 2023, is missing.
> - **RTI Act, Second Schedule, entry 8:** it reads "Special Frontier Force of the Cabinet Secretariat". The 2005 Act printed "Special Prontier Force", and G.S.R. 319(E) of 2021 substituted only the word "Frontier". We could not find an instrument that added "of the Cabinet Secretariat".
> - **RTI Act, s. 13:** the heading reads "Terms of office and conditions of service" where the Act has "Term". A proviso beginning "Provided also that" remains after the new sub-section (5), although s. 2(c) of the RTI (Amendment) Act, 2019 substituted the whole sub-section.
> - **The Constitution, Preamble, footnote 2:** it quotes the replaced words as "Unity of the Nation"; s. 2(b) of the Constitution (Forty-second Amendment) Act, 1976 has "unity of the Nation".
>
> Thank you for your time. I would be glad to share the project's method or data if useful.
>
> Yours faithfully,
> [Your name]
> [Institution, department]
> [Phone]
